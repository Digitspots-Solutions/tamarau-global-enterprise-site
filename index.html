About Us

Sheba Blum Treasury Technology Co (also operating as Sheba Blum Corporate Payments Limited) is a Nigerian fintech company specializing in treasury technology and corporate payment solutions. Founded and led by a certified KYC/AML specialist, we bring deep regulatory expertise to every transaction. Based in Yenagoa, Bayelsa State, we serve businesses across Nigeria with cutting-edge payment processing, treasury management, and compliance services. Our mission is to simplify complex corporate financial operations while maintaining the highest standards of security and regulatory compliance. We understand the unique challenges Nigerian businesses face in managing corporate payments, and we've built our solutions specifically to address these needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is KYC/AML and why is it important?

Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) are regulatory requirements that protect businesses and the financial system from fraud and illegal activities. Our specialist expertise ensures your business remains fully compliant with Nigerian regulations.

How quickly can we get started?

Most businesses can begin processing payments within 3-5 business days after completing our KYC verification process. We'll guide you through every step.

What types of businesses do you serve?

We work with SMEs, corporations, NGOs, and government contractors across all industries in Nigeria. If you need secure corporate payment processing, we can help.

Are your services CBN-compliant?

Yes. All our solutions are designed to meet Central Bank of Nigeria regulations and international financial standards.

What are your transaction limits?

Transaction limits vary based on your business verification level and needs. Contact us for customized limits that match your business requirements.

Do you charge setup fees?

Our pricing structure is transparent and tailored to your business size and transaction volume. Contact us for a detailed quote.

Can you integrate with our existing accounting software?

Yes. We offer API integration with most major accounting and ERP systems used in Nigeria.
